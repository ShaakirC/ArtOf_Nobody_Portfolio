About

Ten years of making things that aren't there look like they always were.

I'm ArtOf_Nobody, a freelance VFX and CGI artist working with production companies, agencies and real estate developers who need a shot fixed, an asset built, or a space imagined before it's poured in concrete.

My background is in compositing, which means every discipline I've picked up since — 3D and motion graphics — gets judged by the same standard: does it sit believably in the frame. I run projects solo end-to-end, or slot into a larger pipeline as one more artist on the crew.
