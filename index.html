About

The somebody behind Nobody.

I'm Shaakir Cassiem, a freelance VFX and CGI artist working under the name ArtOf_Nobody.

I came to 3D by way of a degree in robotics, and I still approach every project like a system: understand the whole, break it into parts, and make sure every piece works together. That means I'm as comfortable planning scope and pipeline as I am solving the technical problem buried in shot forty-two. Big projects don't intimidate me, and unfamiliar ones are my favourite kind.

That curiosity has taken me in a lot of directions. I've produced music, played guitar and keys, built apps, tinkered with DIY electronics, and I'm still deep in game development. Every one of them feeds the work. Music taught me timing, which shows up in every animation I make, and years of coaxing hardware and code into behaving means technical challenges feel like puzzles rather than roadblocks.

Today I work across VFX & compositing, 3D & CGI, and motion graphics, and I'm expanding into interactive 3D websites and virtual tours. This site is part of that. If your project needs both a creative eye and a technical mind, let's talk.
